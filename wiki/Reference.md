**Example resources:** [cohort](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/cohort), [dashboard](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/dashboard)

**Example resources:** [lexicon_tag](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/lexicon_tag), [property_definition](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/property_definition)

**Example resources:** [experiment](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/experiment), [feature_flag](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/feature_flag)

**Example resources:** [warehouse_source](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/warehouse_source), [connector](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/connector)

**Example resources:** [heat_map](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/heat_map), [playlist](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/playlist)

Service accounts, custom roles, and workspace configuration.  
**Example resources:** [service_account](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/service_account), [custom_role](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/mixpanel/mixpanel/latest/docs/resources/custom_role)
